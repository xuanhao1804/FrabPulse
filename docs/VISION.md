# FrabPulse — Product Vision & Philosophy

> **"Understand what moves, when it moves."**

---

## 1. What is FrabPulse?

**FrabPulse** is a **Real-time Event Intelligence Platform**.

- **Frab** = Frog + Lab (curious, experimental, rigorous, observant)
- **Pulse** = Detecting the heartbeat and velocity of markets, events, policy, and technology.

The brand persona is:
**Fintech Dashboard × Research Lab × Modern AI Product.**
It carries a subtle, intelligent, playful scientist-frog motif (lab beakers, wave spectrograms, emerald pulse indicators), but maintains strict financial-grade data credibility.

---

## 2. The Core Problem & Thesis

Most market platforms merely answer:
> *"What is the price right now?"*

Traditional financial news portals answer:
> *"Here is an article claiming why the price moved, written 3 hours later with unverified narratives."*

FrabPulse exists to answer five foundational questions simultaneously:
1. **What just happened?** (Events, announcements, geopolitical shifts, macro releases)
2. **What changed in the data around the same time?** (Price delta, spread divergence, volume spikes)
3. **What are trustworthy, verified sources saying about it?** (Direct source attribution with publication timestamps)
4. **How did the event develop over time?** (Timeline clustering and narrative progression)
5. **What is the empirical temporal correlation between the event and asset movement?**

---

## 3. The Cardinal Rule: Separation of Epistemological Layers

The single most critical design rule in FrabPulse is the strict separation between:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. OBSERVED FACTS                                           │
│    - Mathematical price data (before, after, delta %, time) │
│    - Verifiable timestamps, spreads, official rates         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. SOURCE INTERPRETATIONS                                   │
│    - Quotations and claims made by Reuters, Bloomberg, etc. │
│    - Direct attribution with links and exact publish times  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. AI-GENERATED SYNTHESIS                                   │
│    - Structured extraction of entities, topics, timelines   │
│    - Source-grounded summaries (NEVER hallucinated causes)  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Negative Example (What FrabPulse NEVER does):
> ❌ *"Gold plummeted 1.2% today because the Federal Reserve expressed hawkish concerns over persistent inflation."*
*(This asserts an unproven causal link as an objective truth.)*

### FrabPulse Pattern (What FrabPulse ALWAYS does):
> ✅ **Observed Fact:**
> *"XAU/USD declined 0.82% ($2,384.10 → $2,364.50) within a 35-minute window following the 18:00 UTC FOMC Statement release."*
>
> ✅ **Source Interpretations:**
> *"Reuters and Bloomberg attributed this movement to market adjustments around delayed interest-rate cut expectations."*
>
> ✅ **AI Synthesis:**
> *"Key drivers highlighted across 3 reporting sources: (1) higher terminal rate projections, (2) US Dollar index (DXY) strengthening 0.4%, (3) domestic VN gold (SJC) spread widening to 2.2M VND."*

**Correlation is measured factually. Causation is attributed to reporting entities, never stated as unassisted platform fact.**

## 4. Long-Term Vertical Architecture

FrabPulse is architected for multiple intelligence verticals:

```text
FrabPulse Core
│
├── Gold Pulse (MVP)
│   ├── Vietnamese Domestic Gold (SJC, DOJI, PNJ)
│   ├── International Gold (XAU/USD)
│   ├── Domestic Forex (USD/VND)
│   └── Vietnam vs. World Gold Gap Analysis
│
├── Market Pulse (Future)
│   ├── Equities (VN-Index, S&P 500)
│   ├── Macro Indicators (DXY, Fed Funds Rate, CPI)
│   └── Crypto & Commodities (BTC, Crude Oil)
│
└── AI Pulse (Future)
    ├── Frontier Model Releases & Benchmarks
    ├── API Pricing & Rate Limit Changes
    └── Developer-impacting Infrastructure Outages
```

### Why Gold Pulse First?
Gold is the ultimate testing ground for event intelligence:
- It responds instantly to global macro events (Fed, inflation, wars).
- It has unique localized dynamics (in Vietnam, SJC gold spreads and government import quotas create substantial arbitrage gaps against world spot gold).
- It combines rapid tick data with deep, multi-source narrative reporting.

---

## 5. Summary

**FrabPulse is not a price tracker with AI attached.**  
**FrabPulse is an event-intelligence system that connects real-world events, trustworthy sources, and changing data over time.**
